# Experiment: short descriptive title

- **Hypothesis:** What changes, and why might it help?
- **Domain:** Types, sizes, ranges, exceptional inputs, and assumptions.
- **Accuracy:** Exact result or explicit numerical error requirement.
- **Baselines:** Competitive implementations with the same output contract.
- **Decision rule:** Required correctness and repeatable performance gain.
- **First experiment:** Smallest useful test, preferably under ten minutes.
- **Included costs:** Allocations, conversions, transfers, preparation, and compilation.

## Validation

Reference or proof, difficult cases, randomized sampling, compiler versions and flags.

## Measurements

Commands, hardware, warmups, repetitions, order randomization, raw evidence files.

## Outcome

Accepted within a stated scope, rejected with a reason, or inconclusive. Record uncertainty and counterexamples.

## Challenge and next action

Fresh inputs, stronger baselines, prior-art check, and the next experiment.
